Refund & Cancellation Policy

If this race is canceled due to fire or the ongoing pandemic, no refunds will be given, and your race entry fee will be considered a charitable donation. It will go partially to FBA funds for upcoming events, and also to various other resources, e.g. Felton Fire and Zayante Fire, and Valley Community Resources.

Race t-shirts will still need to be picked up in this case.

This is the same refund/cancellation language included in the event waiver you agree to when registering. You can read the full waiver, including the liability release and medical consent sections, on RunSignUp:
